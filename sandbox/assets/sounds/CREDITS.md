# Recorded sound sources

All sources below are CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Downloaded from the Freesound high-quality MP3 previews and edited to mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV.

- pistol_shot: michorvath, https://freesound.org/people/michorvath/sounds/427592/
- rifle_shot: michorvath, https://freesound.org/people/michorvath/sounds/427598/
- shotgun_shot: michorvath, https://freesound.org/people/michorvath/sounds/427595/
- pistol_reload: nioczkus, https://freesound.org/people/nioczkus/sounds/396331/
- rifle_mechanisms: wadaltmon, https://freesound.org/people/wadaltmon/sounds/263513/
- shotgun_load: Siirxe, https://freesound.org/people/Siirxe/sounds/728613/
- shotgun_pump: TotallyPhilip, https://freesound.org/people/TotallyPhilip/sounds/755495/
- vehicle_crash: magnuswaker, https://freesound.org/people/magnuswaker/sounds/592388/

Shots: trimmed leading silence, shortened reflections with an exponential tail, peak at 0.97. Reload: edited mechanical events placed at the magazine/shell and action animation timings, peak at 0.8. Vehicle crash: dedicated crunch and glass/debris tail, peak at 0.97. The generic metal impact remains procedural.
